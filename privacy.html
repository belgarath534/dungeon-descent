Privacy Policy

Leo Productions  ·  Effective Date: June 24, 2025

1. Overview

Dungeon Descent is a free, accessibility-first dungeon RPG developed by Leo Productions. This Privacy Policy explains what information the game collects, how it is used, and what choices you have. We collect as little data as possible.

2. Information We Collect

A. Guest Mode (no account)

All game data is stored locally on your device only. This includes your character name, class, race, stats, dungeon progress, achievements, inventory, settings, and music preferences. This data never leaves your device.

B. Optional Account (Google Sign-In)

Account creation is entirely optional. If you sign in with Google, we collect:

  • Your email address
  • A display name or username you choose
  • Your cloud-saved game progress (characters, achievements, settings)
  • Tournament scores you choose to submit

This information is stored securely in Firebase and used only to provide cloud saves and leaderboard features.

C. Obituary Feed

When your character dies, the following is posted to a global Obituary visible to all players: character name, class, dungeon, floor, cause of death, kill count, gold collected, and date. No personally identifiable information is included.

3. In-App Purchases

Dungeon Descent offers optional in-app purchases in the form of Mystery Coin packs. These are consumable purchases processed entirely through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment information. All billing is handled by Apple or Google directly. Consumable purchases are non-refundable and cannot be restored.

4. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Your real name
  • Your location or GPS data
  • Your device contacts, camera, or microphone
  • Your phone number
  • Advertising identifiers or behavioral tracking data

5. How We Use Your Information

  • To save your progress across devices when signed in
  • To display your score on the global leaderboard (only if you submit it)
  • To show the global Obituary feed of recent player deaths
  • To power real-time multiplayer features and live events
  • To let you recover your account if you forget your password

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party for advertising or marketing purposes.

6. Third-Party Services

Dungeon Descent uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policy:

  • Firebase Authentication & Firestore (Google) — account login and cloud save storage. Privacy policy
  • Firebase Realtime Database (Google) — real-time multiplayer features and live event data (Void Reaper). Privacy policy
  • Supabase — global obituary database. Privacy policy
  • Cloudflare R2 — audio file storage and delivery. No personal data is included in audio requests beyond a standard IP address. Privacy policy
  • Railway — multiplayer server hosting. Privacy policy

7. Data Retention and Deletion

If you have an account, you can delete it at any time from Account Settings inside the game. Deleting your account removes all cloud-saved data from Firebase. Local device data must be cleared separately through your device settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Dungeon Descent is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Security

We use Firebase's built-in security features to protect account data. Local game data is protected by your device's own security. We take reasonable steps to protect the information we handle but cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top. Continued use of the game after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
