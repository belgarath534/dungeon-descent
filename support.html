Frequently Asked Questions

Progress is saved to your device and your Google account if you're signed in. If you cleared your browser data or reinstalled the app, local saves may be lost. Sign in with Google before starting a run to keep your progress backed up across devices.
Try refreshing the page or restarting the app. If you're on the web version, clearing your browser cache usually fixes loading issues. Make sure you have a stable internet connection — some game assets load from the server on first launch.
Check that your device isn't on silent mode and that volume is turned up. On iOS, make sure the mute switch on the side of your phone is off. In-game, tap the music icon in the top bar to open the music player and make sure a track is selected.
Achievements require an active internet connection to sync. Make sure you're signed in with Google. On iOS, Game Center must be enabled in your device settings. If an achievement still doesn't unlock after meeting the requirement, try restarting the app — it will re-check your progress on next launch.
If your purchase went through but gems didn't appear, go to the shop and tap "Restore Purchases." This re-applies any completed transactions. If the problem persists, contact us below with your order confirmation number and we'll sort it out.
Dungeon Descent is built with accessibility as a core feature, not an afterthought. Every button, combat action, enemy stat, and inventory item is labeled for screen readers. On iOS, VoiceOver is automatically detected and the game switches to native speech output. On Android, TalkBack works through the standard accessibility layer. If something isn't reading correctly, let us know exactly what it is and we'll fix it.
The Shadow World is the 9th dungeon and unlocks after completing The Abyssal Deep. Reach the final floor of The Abyssal Deep and defeat its boss — The Shadow World will appear on the dungeon selection screen after that run ends.
Use the contact form below! Bug reports are especially helpful when you include what dungeon you were in, what floor, and what happened. Feature suggestions are always welcome — this game is built by one developer and player feedback genuinely shapes what gets added.
