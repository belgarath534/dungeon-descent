Privacy Policy

Dungeon Descent

Developed by Leo Productions  |  Effective Date: June 24, 2025

1. Overview

Dungeon Descent is a free, accessibility-first dungeon RPG developed by Leo Productions. This Privacy Policy explains what information the game collects, how it is used, and what choices you have. We take your privacy seriously and collect as little data as possible.

2. Information We Collect

The game collects different types of information depending on whether you create an account or play as a guest.

A. If you play without an account (Guest Mode)

All game data is stored locally on your device only. This includes:

  • Your character name, class, race, and stats
  • Your dungeon progress, achievements, and inventory
  • Your settings and music preferences
  • Local high scores and graveyard records

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

B. If you create an account (Optional)

Account creation is entirely optional. If you choose to sign in with email or Google, we collect:

  • Your email address
  • A display name or username you choose
  • Your cloud-saved game progress (characters, achievements, settings)
  • Tournament scores you choose to submit

This information is stored securely in Firebase (Google) and is used only to provide cloud saves and leaderboard features.

C. Gameplay data shared with other players

When your character dies, the following information is automatically posted to a global Obituary visible to all players:

  • Character name
  • Character class
  • The dungeon and floor where death occurred
  • What killed the character
  • Number of kills and gold collected
  • Date of death

No personally identifiable information (such as your email or real name) is included in obituary posts. The obituary is powered by Supabase.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect any of the following:

  • Your real name
  • Your location or GPS data
  • Your device contacts, camera, or microphone
  • Your phone number
  • Payment information (the game is free with no in-app purchases at this time)
  • Advertising identifiers or behavioral tracking data
  • Any data from children — see Section 7 below

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect only to operate the game:

  • To save your progress across devices when you are signed in
  • To display your score on the global leaderboard (only if you submit it)
  • To show the global Obituary feed of recent player deaths
  • To let you recover your account if you forget your password

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

Dungeon Descent uses the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and cloud save storage. Privacy policy
  • Supabase — global obituary database and audio file storage. Privacy policy

Audio files stream from Supabase storage. No personal data is included in these requests beyond a standard IP address sent with any internet connection.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

If you create an account, you can delete it at any time from within the game by going to Account Settings and selecting Delete Account. Deleting your account removes all cloud-saved data from Firebase. Local data on your device is not affected and must be cleared separately through your device settings.

Guest players can clear all local data at any time by clearing app storage in their device settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Dungeon Descent is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The optional account system requires an email address, which we do not knowingly collect from users under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has created an account, please contact us at the email below and we will delete the account promptly.

8. Security

We use Firebase's built-in security features to protect account data. Local game data is stored in your device's standard app storage and is protected by your device's own security. We cannot guarantee absolute security but take reasonable steps to protect the information we handle.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this document. Continued use of the game after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
